## Art pass before you finish

A preview that passes the gate is not the end of this round. Players judge the game from its
first frame, so finish with one art pass:

1. Capture the game and open the frames yourself.
2. Write down every visual defect you can see. Check that no text overlaps, is clipped or runs
   off-screen. Check that no sprite is a bare rectangle or circle. Check that the background
   is not a flat fill or a plain grid.
3. Fix them:
   - draw characters, enemies, items and props procedurally, with recognisable silhouettes,
     at least two tones of shading and an outline;
   - give the scene depth with layers, a gradient sky or floor, and some texture or variation;
   - keep one palette that fits the theme;
   - lay out HUD and shop labels so that each has its own space, with clear contrast.
4. Capture again, compare with the first frames, and only then submit the final preview.

Report the art pass with `report_progress` so the creator sees why the round took longer.
