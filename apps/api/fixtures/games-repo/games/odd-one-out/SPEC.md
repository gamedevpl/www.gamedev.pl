## Concept

One tile in the grid is a slightly different shade. Find it before the clock runs out. Each
correct pick adds time, grows the grid, and narrows the difference — so it gets harder in two
ways at once. A fixture game, here to give local development a second, quieter thing to play.

## How it plays

Tap the odd tile. A hit adds a few seconds and advances the level; a miss costs time. The
grid starts at two by two and the colour gap shrinks every level, until telling the tiles
apart is genuinely hard. Pointer and keyboard both work, so it plays on a phone or a desk.
