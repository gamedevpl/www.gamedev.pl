## Concept

Blocks fall, you do not get hit. Score is the time you survive, and the fall speed climbs the
whole way, so every run ends — the question is only how long you lasted. A fixture game: it
exists so local development has something real to browse, play, and restart.

## How it plays

Your runner sits at the bottom of the playfield and follows your thumb (or the arrow keys).
Falling blocks spawn faster and drop quicker as the clock runs. One hit ends the run, and the
game-over screen restarts on a tap or a key — no keyboard-only dead end.
